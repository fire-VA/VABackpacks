## 1.0.95
- Staff attack styles (off by default): each early magic staff gets its own primary attack and a secondary attack.
- Placed staff attacks show a ring on the ground first; aim it and left click to cast, right click to cancel.
- Homing staff bolts lock onto the enemy closest to your crosshair.
- Staff attacks no longer hurt your group or your companions.
- Staffs that spawn a Mistile on impact now keep it alive until it reaches its target.
- Held staff effects now take the colour of the staff's gem.

## 1.0.80
- A bag's own speed modifier now applies while you wear it.
- A bag that can't be teleported now blocks portals while it holds anything, worn or carried.
- One camp per character is now enforced by the server.

## 1.0.79
- Fixes and improvements.

## 1.0.78
- Early magic staffs (off by default), each with its own projectile, primary and secondary attacks, held effects, animations and icons.
- Staff bolts no longer hit you or friendly targets.
- Bags looted from chests can roll rarity; bag bonuses also lighten the bag's contents.
- Camp deployment sits properly on edited and voxel terrain, and packing up a camp puts the ground back exactly.
- A worn bag shows on your back next to a cape, and companions can wear backpacks too.
- Fixes for in-game config changes and staff errors.
- Requires FiresUnifiedCore 0.2.580.

- Workbench upgrades with special slots.
