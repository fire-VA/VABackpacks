## 1.0.155
Requires Valheim 1.0.30 or newer.
- Works with Valheim 1.0.30 (camp pack-up reads the new game data).

## 1.0.154

### Changed
- Less memory churn when the game weighs or reads backpack contents. Backpacks behave the same.

## 1.0.153
### Changed
- Less work in the background: the camp counter only draws when shown, the camp chest stops checking once loaded, and building pieces load a little faster. New [Performance] settings, on by default.
- Quieter log: routine messages only show with debug logging on.

## 1.0.146
- Staff icons are now drawn from each staff's own model, framed on the head with the gem lit in the staff's colour ("Rendered staff icons", on by default; off uses the old pictures). (UI)
- The magnet bag upgrade pulls every picked-up item into the bag again after the latest Valheim update. (fix)
- Every staff's left click fires its normal bolt again with the one-handed cast, and the bolt flies and hits. (magic)
- Hold the middle mouse button to aim the ring spell and release to cast it; left click no longer casts the ring spell by mistake, and blocking, Escape, right click or switching staff cancels the aim. (magic)

## 1.0.135
- Lower memory use: the heaviest bag models load only when a bag is worn or dropped (no change in look).
- Chef upgrade works again; its setting is now "Chef I/II/III stations" (old value moved over).
- The auto-feed upgrade is now called Feedbag (existing bags keep it).
- Security: admin status, settings and camp limits only come from the server.

## 1.0.129
- Bug fixes and improvements.
- Staff attack styles (off by default): each early magic staff gets its own primary attack and a secondary attack.
- Placed staff attacks show a ring on the ground first; aim it and left click to cast, right click to cancel.
- Homing staff bolts lock onto the enemy closest to your crosshair.
- Staff attacks no longer hurt your group or your companions.
- Staffs that spawn a Mistile on impact now keep it alive until it reaches its target.
- Held staff effects now take the colour of the staff's gem.

## 1.0.80
- A bag's speed setting now actually changes your speed while you wear it.
- A bag set as not teleportable now blocks portals while it holds anything, worn or carried.
- The one-camp-per-character rule now holds on servers too.
