# VABackpacks

Real backpacks with real storage for Valheim.

- **Five bags.** The Small Bag, Day Bag and Explorer Bag wear on your shoulder next to your cape. The Hobo Stick and the
  Wizard Staff carry their load in hand. Each is crafted at the workbench at its biome's tier, and the shoulder bags
  can turn up in location chests of their biome.
- **The contents travel with the bag.** Everything is stored in the bag item itself: give it to a friend, put it in a
  chest or drop it, and the contents go along. On death the bag lands in your tombstone with its contents intact.
- **Upgrade at the workbench.** A quality 2 bag gets special slots beside its grid: a tool slot (one weapon or tool), a
  resources slot (one stack that weighs a quarter as much) and, on the bigger bags, a teleport bypass slot that lets one
  non-teleportable stack through portals.
- **Bags become camps.** Drop any bag and unpack it into a camp: campfire, the bag standing open as your chest,
  workbench, bed and more. Pack it back up when you leave.
- **Bag perks**, set by the server per bag: cold and wet resistance, feather fall, extra carry strength.

Works on its own, or with VAInventory, which shows the worn bag's grid inside the inventory panel. Keys and details
are on the in-game help page.

Recipes, grid sizes, loot chance and perks are server-synced config (`com.Fire.VA_Backpacks.cfg`).

Requires FiresUnifiedCore. Part of the Fires mod family.
