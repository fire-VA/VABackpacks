## 1.0.94
- Staff attack styles (off by default): each early magic staff gets its own primary attack and a secondary attack.
- Placed staff attacks show a ring on the ground first; aim it and left click to cast, right click to cancel.
- Homing staff bolts lock onto the enemy closest to your crosshair.
- Staff attacks no longer hurt your group or your companions.
- Staffs that spawn a Mistile on impact now keep it alive until it reaches its target.
- Held staff effects now take the colour of the staff's gem.

## 1.0.80
- A bag's speed setting now actually changes your speed while you wear it.
- A bag set as not teleportable now blocks portals while it holds anything, worn or carried.
- The one-camp-per-character rule now holds on servers too.

## 1.0.79
- Fixes and improvements.

## 1.0.78
- Added early magic staffs (off by default), each with its own attacks, effects and icons.
- Staff bolts no longer hit you or your friends.
- Bags found in chests can roll a rarity, and bag bonuses also lighten what's inside.
- Camps sit properly on uneven or edited ground, and packing up puts the ground back exactly.
- A worn bag shows on your back next to a cape, and companions can wear backpacks too.
- Fixed errors when changing settings in game and when using staffs.

## 1.0.48
- Added recipes and chest loot for bags.
- Added workbench upgrades with special slots.
- Bags now have readable names.
